### 2.2 How to Approach Sponsors — Step by Step

**Step 1: Research & List**
- Make a spreadsheet: Company Name, Contact Person, Email, LinkedIn, Phone, Tier Target
- Find the right person (NOT generic info@ emails):
  - Search LinkedIn: "[Company] Marketing Manager" or "CSR Head" or "Partnerships Lead"
  - Check company website for partnership/sponsorship page
  - For startups: directly message the founder on LinkedIn/Twitter

**Step 2: First Contact — Cold Email**
```
Subject: Partnership Opportunity — Vishvakarma Innovation Challenge 2026 (National Level, Tirupati)

Dear [Name],

I'm [Your Name], Founder of Trinetrashakti Innovations Pvt Ltd. We're organizing the 
Vishvakarma Innovation Challenge 2026 — a national-level startup & innovation competition 
being held in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh this July.

Event highlights:
• 5,000+ expected participants from 100+ colleges across India
• Categories: AI/ML, FinTech, HealthTech, AgriTech, and 10+ more
• 10,00,000+ digital reach across social media
• Live finals with investor pitching on 23 July 2026
• Platform: www.vishvakarmahub.com

We believe [Company Name] aligns perfectly with our mission of nurturing grassroots 
innovation. I'd love to explore a partnership that gives your brand direct access to 
India's next generation of builders and founders.

I've attached our partnership deck. Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Founder, Trinetrashakti Innovations Pvt Ltd
[Phone] | [Email]
www.vishvakarmahub.com
```

**Step 3: Follow-Up Schedule**
- Day 1: Send email + LinkedIn connection request
- Day 3: LinkedIn message (if no response)
- Day 7: Follow-up email: "Just circling back on my previous email..."
- Day 14: Final follow-up or phone call
- Rule: 3 follow-ups maximum. If no response, move on.

**Step 4: The Meeting**
- Keep it to 15-20 minutes
- Show the platform live (vishvakarmahub.com/competition)
- Show the sponsor packages page — "Partner With Us to Grow Your Business"
- Customize the pitch per company:
  - Tech company → "access 500+ tech-savvy innovators for recruitment"
  - FinTech → "sponsor the FinTech challenge track"
  - Education → "brand visibility in 100+ colleges"
- Be ready with: invoice, GST details, MoU template

**Step 5: Close the Deal**
- Send MoU/Agreement within 24 hours of verbal confirmation
- Collect advance payment (50% upfront, 50% before event)
- Add sponsor to website immediately (admin panel → Sponsors)
- Send them branding guidelines and deliverables list

**DO NOT** send to generic college email (principal@). It gets lost.

**Start Date for Government Outreach: By 25 April 2026 (90 days before finals)**

### 5.1 How to Invite the Prime Minister

**Who:** Hon'ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi

**Process:**
1. Write a formal invitation letter on company letterhead (Trinetrashakti Innovations Pvt Ltd)
2. Address to:
   ```
   The Private Secretary to the Prime Minister
   Prime Minister's Office (PMO)
   South Block, Raisina Hill
   New Delhi – 110011
   ```
3. Send via:
   - **Speed Post** (keep receipt)
   - **Email:** connect@mygov.nic.in or through PMO website
   - **PMO Portal:** https://www.pmindia.gov.in → "Write to PM" / Invitation section
   - Through your **local MP** (Tirupati MP) — ask them to forward
4. Also write to the **Ministry of Education** and **Ministry of Skill Development** — if they forward to PMO, it carries more weight

**Letter Format:**
```
[Company Letterhead]

Date: [Date]

To,
The Private Secretary to the Hon'ble Prime Minister of India
Prime Minister's Office
South Block, Raisina Hill, New Delhi – 110011

Subject: Invitation to Grace the Grand Finale of Vishvakarma Innovation 
Challenge 2026 — A National Level Innovation Competition

Respected Sir/Madam,

Trinetrashakti Innovations Pvt Ltd is organizing the "Vishvakarma Innovation 
Challenge 2026," a national-level startup and innovation competition being held 
in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh on 23rd July 2026.

The event aligns with the Hon'ble Prime Minister's vision of "Startup India" 
and "Skill India" — bringing together 5,000+ students and young entrepreneurs 
from 100+ colleges across India to compete, innovate, and build solutions for 
real-world problems.

Event Details:
- Event: Vishvakarma Innovation Challenge 2026
- Date: 23rd July 2026
- Venue: [Venue Name], Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh
- Expected Participation: 5,000+ innovators, 100+ colleges
- Categories: AI/ML, FinTech, HealthTech, AgriTech, CleanTech, and more
- Website: www.vishvakarmahub.com

We humbly request the Hon'ble Prime Minister to grace the event as the 
Chief Guest. His presence would inspire thousands of young innovators and 
send a powerful message about India's commitment to grassroots innovation.

We kindly request a video message or a virtual address if an in-person 
visit is not possible due to schedule constraints.

Enclosed:
1. Event Brochure
2. Company Profile
3. Competition Overview Document

With profound respect,

[Your Name]
Founder & CEO
Trinetrashakti Innovations Pvt Ltd
[Phone] | [Email]
[Company Address]
```

**Reality Check:** PM's personal presence is extremely unlikely for a first-edition event. However:
- You might get a **congratulatory letter** or **video message** which you can use in marketing
- The attempt itself adds credibility when approaching other officials

## KEY PRINCIPLES

1. **Start with local** — Tirupati colleges, Tirupati companies (Amara Raja!), District Collector
2. **Personal visits > emails** for local targets
3. **Government invitations take time** — start 90 days before
4. **Sponsors need proof** — show them the live platform, registration numbers, social media
5. **Campus ambassadors are your army** — incentivize them well
6. **Never stop promoting** — daily social media, weekly email updates
7. **Document everything** — photos, videos, testimonials for next edition
8. **Underpromise, overdeliver** — especially to sponsors
9. **Have a Plan B** — backup venue, backup judges, backup AV
10. **The event is 20% stage, 80% preparation** — nail the groundwork
